# 🎓 Students Behavior Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes student behavior, mental health, and academic patterns using an interactive dashboard. The goal is to uncover insights that can help improve student performance and well-being.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### 🔹 Overview

* Total Students: 10,000
* Average Age: 23
* Avg Attendance: 77%
* Avg Study Hours: 4.5 hrs
* Avg Sleep Hours: 6.5 hrs

### 🔹 Demographics

* Balanced gender distribution
* Majority of students aged **21–24**
* Most students are **senior year**

### 🔹 Academic Insights

* BBA has the highest number of students
* Attendance is below optimal levels
* Study vs sleep imbalance observed

---

## 🧠 Mental Health Insights

* Avg Stress Score: 4.93 / 10
* Avg Anxiety Score: 4.66 / 10
* Most students have **medium anxiety (65%)**
* Seniors experience the **highest stress levels**
* Mood distribution shows majority are **neutral**, followed by calm

---

## 🏃 Lifestyle Insights

* Physical activity reduces stress levels (negative correlation)
* Avg screen time: 25%
* Avg caffeine intake: 2.5 / 5
* Social support: 56%

---

## 💡 Key Findings

* 📈 Higher exam pressure → higher stress
* 🧑‍🎓 Senior students are the most affected group
* 🏃 Active students tend to have lower stress
* 🚫 Many students take no action to manage stress
